A row count that disagrees with the glyph count is the first sign the two have drifted -- TREE8 pins six. Reuses the icm--* masked-icon classes rather than adding six #bi-* sprite symbols: the legend renders a BOUNDED count (once per page), so either mechanism is fine per the builder.css comment, and reusing icm--* keeps the two id sets (bi-* vs icm--*) distinct so TREE8 and the tree tests cannot collide. TWO structural decisions, both deliberate: 1. It is a CLOSED
. As six always-on rows it sat as a wrapping band of grey glyph+label pairs directly above the tree and read as a row of checkboxes -- permanent screen cost for something a CA learns once. The root class and the six .flag-legend__row children are unchanged, so the structural pins (TREE8's count, the stylesheet guard) still hold. 2. The six rows are SPLIT INTO THE TWO AXES they belong to. Published/Draft and Required/Optional are independent settings, not one three-state control -- that is the design's central constraint, and a flat list of six actively hid it. Grouping is the cheapest place to teach it. `aria-hidden` on the glyph span: the label text beside it already carries the meaning; a bare masked box has nothing for a screen reader to read.{% endcomment %}
