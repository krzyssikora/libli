{% load i18n %} {% comment %}The caption toolbar: a deliberate SUBSET of _rte_toolbar.html. A caption is one line of prose, so no headings, lists, quote, code, alignment or math -- and no colour swatches. Every button here has a matching tag in CAPTION_TAGS (courses/sanitize.py); a button whose output the sanitiser deletes would be a control that silently does nothing, so the two are pinned together by test_caption_editor.test_the_caption_toolbar_offers_nothing_the_sanitiser_would_strip. No JS of its own: applyCmd in text_toolbar.js dispatches on data-cmd and simply never sees the commands this toolbar omits.{% endcomment %}
