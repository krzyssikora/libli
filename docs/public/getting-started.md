# Getting started

libli is a learning platform run just for your school, on a server dedicated to it: teachers
build courses out of lessons and quizzes, students work through them, and the marking, progress
tracking and analytics come with it.

## Trying to log in?

- **You do not create your own account.** On a school deployment the school creates accounts and
  the sign-in page tells you to ask your administrator. If your sign-in page instead offers a
  "Sign up" link, self-registration has been switched on for this site and you can use it.
- **Forgotten password.** Go to [/accounts/password/reset/](/accounts/password/reset/) and enter
  your email address. This works only if your account has an email address on it — an email
  address is optional in libli and many school accounts have none. If yours does not, your
  administrator has to set a new password for you.
- **Your invitation stopped working.** An invitation link is valid for 14 days from the moment it
  is issued. After that it expires and cannot be revived; ask whoever invited you to send a fresh
  one.
- **Something is broken.** Tell your teacher, who can escalate it, or write to
  {libli:contact_email}.

Signing in is a separate matter from setting libli up for a school in the first place.
