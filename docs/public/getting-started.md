# Getting started

libli is a learning platform run just for your school, on a server dedicated to it: teachers
build courses out of lessons and quizzes, students work through them, and the marking, progress
tracking and analytics come with it.

{libli:demo_notice}

## Trying to log in?

### You do not create your own account

On a school deployment the school creates accounts, and the sign-in page of your school's libli
site tells you to ask your administrator. If your sign-in page instead offers a "Sign up" link,
self-registration has been switched on for this site and you can use it.

{libli:vendor_only}

### I cannot log in to libli.pl

Only the site's administrators and people with two-week demo access (see below) can log in to
libli.pl. It is not a school site. Your school's site is at a different address. If you do not
know it, ask at your school.

{libli:end_vendor_only}

### Forgotten password

Go to [/accounts/password/reset/](/accounts/password/reset/) and enter your email address. This
works only if your account has an email address on it — an email address is optional in libli
and many school accounts have none. If yours does not, your administrator has to set a new
password for you.

### Your invitation stopped working

An invitation link is valid for 14 days from the moment it is issued. After that it expires and
cannot be revived; ask the person who invited you, or your school's administrator, for a new
invitation.

### Something is broken

Tell your teacher, who can escalate it, or write to {libli:contact_email}.

{libli:vendor_only}
